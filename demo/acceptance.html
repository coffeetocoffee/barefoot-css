The wizard — mid-flow

A shared nav owns an empty <form> wrapping the panels: the app moves exactly one aria-current="step" and one panel per click, panels are hidden — never removed, so back preserves input — and leaving a step runs native validation. The stepper contract is exactly one current step on an <li>.
