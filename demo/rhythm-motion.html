.bf-stagger — staggered entry animation

Children animate in sequence with a calculated delay (calc(var(--bf-stagger-index) * 50ms)). Pure CSS via @starting-style and view() timeline. Respects prefers-reduced-motion.

How it works

  • Each child receives a --bf-stagger-index (set by author or JS)
  • Animation delay = calc(index * var(--bf-stagger-step, 50ms))
  • Uses animation-timeline: view() for scroll-triggered entry
  • Falls back to @starting-style + transition for engines without view()
  • prefers-reduced-motion: reduce kills all motion instantly
