Paint & paper

Three small proofs, zero JavaScript: a validation group that tints when its field is wrong, a sticky table that hints at more data, and a print layer that flattens it all to ink on paper. Back to the conformance demo · paint & paper docs.

Validation groups

Wrap a label, a control, and its message in .bf-form-group. Touch the field with a bad value and the whole group tints while .bf-error-text appears — :has(:user-invalid) does it, no script. The message is wired with aria-describedby so it stays announced.

Sticky table

.bf-table-sticky is the scroll container, the pinned header, the pinned leading column, and the fade edge in one class. The wrapper carries tabindex="0" and a name so keyboard users can scroll it.
